# Sophia Kubo — Portfolio

A one-page portfolio for Sophia Kubo, Quality Control, built as a single magazine issue printed as a proof copy. It is a team-up with Sean Soriano's portfolio: a sibling site, not a copy of it.

## Language

### The issue

**Issue**:
The whole site: one long page read top to bottom like a single magazine issue.
_Avoid_: site, page, portfolio (in visitor-facing copy)

**Proof**:
The editorial concept: the Issue is a printer's proof copy, and an editor's red pen is quality control for print. Every section carries the marks of being checked.
_Avoid_: theme, style

**Proof Marks**:
The red-pen proofreading marks laid over the Issue: circled words, margin ticks, strike-throughs that get corrected, and the approval stamp. Kept restrained: one or two per spread.
_Avoid_: annotations, doodles, decorations

**Correction**:
The one deliberate typo in a Cover headline that the red pen strikes through and fixes. It is the Issue's only joke and is never repeated elsewhere.
_Avoid_: typo gag, easter egg

**Bone**:
The warm paper (`#EEE8DF`) the Issue is printed on: a shade warmer than Sean's Premium White, so the two read as related but distinct.
_Avoid_: white, cream, background

**Proof Red**:
The editor's red (`#D3261C`), and the Issue's only accent. It belongs to the Proof Marks and to RedBank, and to nothing else.
_Avoid_: brand red, accent

### Voice

**Pea**:
Sophia's nickname, and the name the third-person copy uses for her, with she/her ("Pea found it before launch; she wrote the test that caught it").
_Avoid_: Sophia (in running copy)

### The sections, in order

**Press Run**:
The opening moment before the Cover: crop marks, registration targets and colour bars draw in, a counter runs to 100, and the Cover is printed into view. Shorter on a repeat visit.
_Avoid_: loader, splash, intro

**Cover**:
The opening spread: Sophia's cut-out portrait on a warm paper studio backdrop, standing in front of the Masthead. The photo stays in full colour.
_Avoid_: hero, landing, header

**Masthead**:
"KUBO", set huge across the Cover behind Sophia's head, with the cover line "Sophia Kubo — Quality Control · The Proof Issue, Nº 01 · 2026".
_Avoid_: logo, title, wordmark

**Contents**:
The numbered index of every Work. Hovering a row swaps the preview image beside it.
_Avoid_: table of contents, menu, work list

**Cover Story**:
RedBank, told as the Issue's long feature across several spreads, with its real screenshots. It is framed as Quality Control: what was checked, what broke, what was re-checked.
_Avoid_: case study, featured project

**Department**:
One of the shorter Work pieces after the Cover Story. For now every Department is a Placeholder Work.
_Avoid_: project card, section

**Method**:
The section on how Pea checks.
_Avoid_: process, skills

**Interview**:
A Q&A with Sophia in first person, opened by the white-background studio portrait.
_Avoid_: about, bio

**Back Cover**:
The closing section: contact details and the Contributors credit.
_Avoid_: footer, contact section

**Contributors**:
The single small credit on the Back Cover linking to Sean Soriano's portfolio. It is the only place the two sites point at each other.
_Avoid_: partner link, team section

### The work

**Quality Control**:
Pea's role: checking what has been built, hands-on, before it ships, the way she checks Sean's work whenever he ships. It is not Quality Assurance, which designs the tests and the process.
_Avoid_: QA, Quality Assurance, tester

**Work**:
A real system Pea checked before it shipped. RedBank is the only real Work so far.
_Avoid_: project (unqualified), portfolio piece

**Placeholder Work**:
A Work with a generic title and stock images, standing in until a real one replaces it.
_Avoid_: dummy, fake project

### The identity

**Loupe**:
Sophia's mark: Sean's glasses glyph mirrored, with a short handle on the steady lens so it reads as a magnifying glass. Shown side by side, the two marks face each other.
_Avoid_: logo, icon, glasses
